Focus lock · Bedtime lock

Lock the apps that pull you away.

Focus lock blocks distracting apps while you work. Bedtime lock keeps them locked at night, until you’re up. You decide once, ahead of time.

Willpower makes a bad lock.

You pick up your phone to check the time, and forty minutes later you’re still scrolling. At night it’s one more video, then another. Saying no every single time is the hard part.

An app blocker says it for you. You decide once, in advance. When the moment comes, the app doesn’t open, and there’s nothing to negotiate.

Focus lock: for the next hour.

For work, studying or anything else that needs your attention for a while.

  • Allow a few, or block a few.

    Keep only what you need, like Messages, Maps and Music, or lock just the apps that pull you away: social, games, video. Websites you pick count the same way.

  • Start it in one tap.

    Tap the lock on Home and pick how long, from 15 minutes to 4 hours. Or press and hold the app icon for 25 or 50 minutes.

  • Or with every routine.

    Turn it on for runs and sidequests. It locks while your routine is on and ends when you’re done.

Bedtime lock: until you’re up.

Plan your nights like alarms: one for weekdays, one for the weekend. 23:00 to 7:00 to start with, and a quiet heads-up half an hour before.

  • Wind down first.

    Before bedtime, Home says nothing needs doing and water nudges stop. Then the apps you picked lock, from your focus list or a list just for the night.

  • Ends when you’re up.

    Your apps unlock at your wake-up time, or as soon as you’ve done your alarm’s wake-up task.

  • Firmer, with an NFC tag.

    Make an NFC tag your key and stick it somewhere you have to get up for, like the bathroom mirror. Then only that tag ends the night.

  • Not Yet stays open.

    It locks the apps you pick, not the whole phone. Leave Clock unlocked so you can set your alarm.

Not Yet never sees your apps.

You pick them in Apple’s own picker, and iOS keeps your choice on your iPhone in a form Not Yet can’t read. No usage data leaves your phone. No account, no tracking in the app.

Questions? Answers.

Can it block apps at night?

Yes. Bedtime lock locks the apps you pick on the nights you planned, until your wake-up time or until your alarm’s task is done. With an NFC tag as your key, only that tag ends the night.

Which apps can I lock?

Any apps and app categories you pick, plus websites. Either lock just those, or lock everything except the few you allow.

Can Not Yet see which apps I use?

No. The picker belongs to iOS, and Not Yet only gets a list it can’t read. Nothing about your app use leaves your iPhone.

Do I need a routine to use the locks?

No. Focus lock and Bedtime lock work on their own. If you do use routines, Focus lock can switch on with every run.

Is it an app blocker for ADHD?

It was built with ADHD in mind: the decision happens once, ahead of time, instead of every time you reach for your phone. It helps anyone who loses evenings to the feed.
