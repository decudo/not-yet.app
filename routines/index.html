Routines · Rotation

An ADHD routine app that changes before it gets old.

Your steps stay the same. The way the routine plays changes every few runs, and you can start it and check off steps without opening the app.

Routine apps get boring. Then they get deleted.

A new routine app feels good for a week or two. Same list, same checkbox, every morning. Then the novelty wears off, a streak breaks, and the app ends up in a folder you never open.

Not Yet keeps your steps and changes the game around them, before your head gets bored.

Same steps. A different game.

Set up your routine once, step by step. Every few runs, the way it plays changes, and never back to one of the last two.

  • How the next step is picked

    Wheel, Dice, Cards, Countdown, Blind run or Boss fight, where the longest step is the boss and comes last.

  • How you check it off

    Stamp the card, tear the Ticket, click the Tally counter, hold until the Rocket lifts off, and more.

  • A twist now and then

    Only half the steps today, a bonus step, or a line to yourself that turns up on the next run.

Start without opening the app.

When it’s time, the routine comes to you.

  • On your Lock Screen

    At its time, your routine shows up with a Start button. Then the current step, with Done right next to it.

  • In a widget

    The next routine or the current step on your Home Screen, checked off with a tap.

  • On your Apple Watch

    Start a run, check off steps and log water from your wrist.

  • With an NFC tag

    Tap the tag on your bathroom mirror, and the routine that belongs there starts.

  • Right after the alarm

    Pick a routine for after your alarm. It starts as soon as the wake-up task is done.

Runs, not streaks.

Every time you do a routine is a run with its own number. All of it, some of it or stopped early: it counts.

  • Coins for every step

    Steps, to-dos, sidequests and glasses of water earn coins. One step per run is golden and earns more.

  • Smash the piggy bank

    At the end of a run, the coins sit in a piggy bank. A few hits, and it breaks.

  • Your own rewards

    Spend coins on what you pick, like a coffee or an episode, or on hammers and piggy skins in the Kiosk.

  • Sidequests for the time in between

    Got 30 minutes? Say how much time you have and get one to-do that fits.

  • A short one after a break

    Back after a few days? Your first run is shorter. Nothing mentions the pause.

Questions? Answers.

How is it different from a habit tracker?

There are no streaks, no percentages and no calendar of gaps. Each run has a number, and whatever you finish counts. What changes is the game, not the score.

Can I check off steps without opening the app?

Yes. At its time, the routine shows up on your Lock Screen. Start it and check off each step there, in a widget or on your Apple Watch.

Which ways to play are there?

Wheel, Dice, Cards, Countdown, Blind run and Boss fight pick the next step. Seven gestures check it off, from a stamp to a rocket. Twists like Only half come on top.

What happens if I skip a few days?

Nothing counts down and nothing turns red. When you come back after a longer break, your first run is a short one.

Is Not Yet a routine app for ADHD?

Yes. It was built with ADHD in mind: short steps, a routine that changes before it gets boring, and no streaks to lose. No diagnosis needed.
