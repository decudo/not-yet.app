Week

A weekly planner without deadlines.

Tap the date on Home to see the next seven days. Drop a to-do into a free spot: roughly then, not a deadline. Nothing turns red when it slips.

Deadlines turn red.

A to-do with a due date is a small promise. Miss it, and it sits there in red, listing what you didn’t do. After a few of those, opening the planner feels worse than not planning at all.

Week works the other way round. You don’t set a deadline, you find a gap: Tuesday, 15:00, about 30 minutes. If it happens, nice. If not, the to-do goes back to your list without a fuss.

The next seven days, at a glance.

Week starts today and shows what’s already there, so you can see where the gaps are.

  • Routines and alarms

    Your morning routine, the evening one, the alarm that wakes you. Each at its time.

  • Events and birthdays

    Appointments from the calendars you pick, and birthdays that turn into a step in that day’s routine.

  • Dots for what’s different

    Under each day, a small dot for a birthday, an appointment or a planned to-do. Ordinary days stay plain.

  • Quiet days stay quiet

    No empty grid, no percentages. A day with nothing on shows its date and when you’re up.

Plan a to-do into a free spot.

Tap “Plan”, pick a to-do, then tap a free time. Or tap the space between two things and see what fits there.

  • Roughly then

    A planned to-do has a time, not a deadline. When it’s time, the reminder shows the to-do’s icon.

  • Only what fits

    Each free spot shows the to-dos short enough for it.

  • Move it, don’t lose it

    Hold a planned to-do to change its time, move it to another day or take it off the week. It stays on your list.

  • Nothing turns red

    If it doesn’t happen, it fades at its time and leaves the day in the evening. No overdue list, no counting.

  • In your calendar app, too

    If you like, planned to-dos also show up in your calendar app, in a calendar of their own.

Got 30 minutes right now?

That’s a sidequest: say how much time you have, and Not Yet picks one to-do that fits. More about routines and sidequests

Questions? Answers.

Is Week a calendar?

No, it sits next to your calendar. Week shows your routines, alarms and planned to-dos, along with events and birthdays from the calendars you pick. You plan to-dos here; appointments stay in your calendar app.

What if I don’t get to a planned to-do?

Nothing bad happens. It fades at its time and leaves the day in the evening. It stays on your to-do list, and nothing is marked overdue.

How far ahead can I plan?

Week shows today and the six days after it. That’s far enough to plan and short enough to take in at a glance.

Is it a weekly planner for ADHD?

It was built with ADHD in mind: no deadlines to miss, nothing red, and a plan you can move with one hold. It works for anyone who likes a loose plan better than a strict one.
